For the pros.

A big part of our business never shows up on the retail lot, and we like it that way.

Restaurants and smokehouses

We supply cooking wood to food-service customers across the region, over a hundred of them. Local pecan and mesquite by the cord, boxed pecan, applewood chunks, and pecan shells for smoking. If your menu depends on fire, talk to us about a standing order so your woodpile is never the thing that runs out.

Landscape contractors and builders

Flagstone by the pallet, railroad ties by the bundle of sixteen, Coronado stone veneer by the box, river rock, and pecan-shell mulch by the cubic yard or the semi-load. We quote by the job, we load you fast, and we deliver on our own trucks.

Volume and wholesale

Pecan shells and firewood are available in truckload quantities, and we have shipped wood well beyond the Mesilla Valley when the order calls for it.

Call (575) 522-2852 and ask for a contractor or volume quote.
