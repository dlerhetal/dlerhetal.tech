We bring it to you.

Our own trucks deliver firewood, flagstone, railroad ties, pecan shells, and large pottery across the region. Delivery runs on our regular routes through Las Cruces, Mesilla, Doña Ana, and El Paso, and for larger loads we range farther out, including Deming, Truth or Consequences, and Elephant Butte. The delivery fee is quoted by location and is separate from the price of the material, so call (575) 522-2852 and we will quote your address before you commit.

[Confirm with Miguel: service area framing. Old site promises Deming and T or C runs; current practice is about a 25 mile radius, longer runs by load size.]

A few things that make firewood delivery go smoothly

  • We drop the wood where you want it, as long as our truck can get there. Plan on about nine feet of clearance.
  • Want it stacked? We can stack within six feet of the truck for a stacking fee. Farther than that carries a carting fee, just tell us when you schedule so we bring the right equipment.
  • Pickup is always free. Drive in during business hours, and bring a truck or trailer.

Smaller items ship nationwide

Smaller items, including pottery under 40 pounds, Talavera, smoking woods, and yard art, ship nationwide with carrier rates calculated at checkout.

Questions about your address or your load? Call (575) 522-2852 and the office will get you a quote.
