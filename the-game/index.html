What these mean

Wall
The bills you have to pay every month no matter what. Rent, payroll, trucks, insurance. The wall you have to clear before you've made a dollar.
Game
The profit you've decided is the floor. Win that and you've won the month.
Goal
What you actually want on top of winning. The stretch.
Steps
Wall + Game + Goal, divided by your working days. The daily tick. Hit it every day and you cross the finish line.
