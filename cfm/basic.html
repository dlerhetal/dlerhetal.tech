CFM Basic

Update the 13-Week Cash Flow Model, the simple weekly routine, one step at a time.
Cash Flow Wizard  |  Written for an owner doing this alone, with no bookkeeping background
Step 1 of 13
1Monday: roll the cash plan forward

One action per screen. Press NEXT when it is done.

You need Excel on a Windows computer, your bank login, and QuickBooks.

Back is always safe. This page remembers where you were.

2Open last week's workbook

Open the newest Cash Flow Model file. The date at the end of its name is last Saturday.

A yellow bar at the top? Click Enable Editing, then Enable Content.

3Copy first. New Week has no undo.

Next you save this week's file under a new name. Last week's file stays untouched, so there is always a way back.

4Save this week's file

Go to File > Save As. Change only the date at the end of the name to:

YYMMDD

That is this coming Saturday, this Saturday, written year, month, day.

Done when the title bar at the top shows the new name.
5Press New Week once. Only once.

A second press rolls a second week and loses dates you entered.

6Press New Week
  • Click the *** CFM : Cash Forecast Model *** tab, then Current Rolling Quarter.
  • Click New Week one time.
  • The box titled Continue?: click Yes.
  • The next box: click OK.

Nothing happens? Macros are off. Reopen the workbook and choose Enable Content.

7Check the first date moved

The first date across the top of the week columns must read:

this Saturday
Still last Saturday? Close without saving, reopen this week's file, and do Step 6 again. Still wrong? Stop and leave a note here.
8Type today's bank money
  • Add up this morning's balance in every business bank account.
  • Click the orange cell, first week, row (A) BEGINNING BALANCE CASH.
  • Type the total. Numbers only. Press Enter.
9Update money coming in
  • In QuickBooks, open the Accounts receivable aging detail report.
  • In the workbook, click Cash Receipts.
  • Put each customer payment in the week you expect it. A promised date wins.
  • More than 90 days late with no promised date? Leave it out.
10Update money going out
  • In QuickBooks, open the Unpaid bills report.
  • In the workbook, click Cash Expenses. Put each bill in the week you will pay it.
  • A big one-off payment goes in whole, in one week.
  • Repeating bills live in Recurring Expenses. Change one only if it changed.
11Find the lowest week

Click Current Rolling Quarter. Read the row (E.1) ENDING CASH AVAILABLE across all 13 weeks.

Every week above zero? You are set.
Any week below zero? Press NEXT.
12A week is below zero
  • Check nothing is in twice or in the wrong week.
  • Ask your biggest late customers for a date. Move their payment to that week.
  • Move a bill only if the payee agreed. Move all of it.
  • Still short? Write the week and the amount in the note below.
13Save and close

Click Current Rolling Quarter, press Ctrl + S, and close Excel.

Leave last week's file alone.

Finished. This week's plan is ready.
