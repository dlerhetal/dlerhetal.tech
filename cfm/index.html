Cash Flow Wizards

Step by step walk throughs for keeping a 13-week cash flow model current, written for the owner who runs the numbers because there is nobody else to run them.

A cash flow model answers one question: will there be enough money in the bank every week for the next quarter, and if not, which week is the tight one. Each wizard is one action per step, tells you how to tell it worked, and keeps your place and your notes in your own browser where only you can see them.
