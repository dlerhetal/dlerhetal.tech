Deep-Dive Findings

Synthesis of four research streams (entity/license, website/digital, permits, competitors/marketing). Full sourced briefings in work\research\; the consolidated master briefing is deep_dive_v2_260912.md.

License & legal — the urgent one

  • Suspended = unlicensed for B&P 7031. Any B-license work performed or collected on while suspended can be clawed back 100% by the customer (disgorgement), even if the work is flawless and even if the customer knew. Highest-dollar risk; reason to pause licensed work now.
  • The cure is fast and clean if Paul stays: reinstate/rescind Paul's $25,000 Bond of Qualifying Individual within the ~90-day window (rescission by roughly 11/13/2026) and the suspension lifts, potentially retroactively, with no exam and no new application.
  • The decisive fork: is Paul leaving, or did the bond just lapse? That single fact chooses the path (bond cure vs. replace the qualifier). If Paul exits, Tyler must qualify himself (4 yrs experience + exams) or bring a real RMO/RME — renting a paper qualifier is a misdemeanor (7068.1).
  • The ~$1,000 minor-work exemption and the mitigation-vs-reconstruction line matter for what they can legally touch meanwhile. This is landscape, not legal advice — route specifics to a CA construction attorney.

Website & digital footprint

  • The website is hacked. quality-restore.com is a compromised WordPress site serving live casino/gambling spam; Google has indexed 500+ injected pages titled "| Quality Restoration Services". Security + brand + SEO-penalty hazard. Tyler is very likely unaware.
  • Dead as a lead source: real service pages last edited 2016–2017, home/about 2022; no city landing pages, no lead-capture form (phone only), no chat/financing/scheduler.
  • Marketing is dormant everywhere: Facebook ~48 followers, last post ~Oct 2022; skeletal LinkedIn; no Instagram/TikTok; no evidence of any paid acquisition.
  • Reputation is split across two entities — the LLC (Tyler, 2024) and Paul's predecessor sole prop (BBB A+ since 2018, separate CSLB license #1046073 now expired). Relevant to the "18 years" claim and the ownership-transition story.

Competitors & marketing

  • The suspension is also a marketing blocker: Google Guaranteed / Local Services Ads and every carrier DRP/TPA program require a license in good standing — so the two highest-volume paid channels are gated until it clears. This sets the sequence for the whole marketing plan.
  • They're losing the "near me" search war to one beatable rival (Superior Restoration, ~367 Google reviews @ 4.9) — and it's an organic/review lead, not paid, so a review-velocity engine can close it with no license dependency.
  • Best growth lever needs no money and no license clearance: a systematic ~2-reviews/week Google cadence. They already earn 5-star work (52 Yelp, 5.0 HomeAdvisor); they're just not routing it to Google, where ranking rewards recency.
  • Referrals (plumbers + insurance agents first) are the cheapest, highest-converting, cash-flow-smoothing channel — and their "we handle your claim" reputation is the exact message that converts, provable with customer quotes.

Permits & the revenue truth

  • Do not size this business from permits. Exactly one permit is attributable anywhere searchable: a $500 interior drywall patch (2022) under the predecessor sole prop. Mitigation/water/mold work is non-permitted, so a near-empty trail is expected and does not undercut the revenue claim.
  • Size revenue from the return / Form 568 / Xactimate exports / AR aging — never permits. The $1.1M vs $1.5M conflict gets resolved from the books, on-site.
  • One high-value source is still unmined: Corona eTRAKiT (their home city, login-gated) — a 10-minute manual browser search by contractor name is the single highest-leverage next step. Shovels.ai (paid) would consolidate all jurisdictions at once by license #.

Questions for Tyler

Built from the findings, ordered by urgency then by the client's stated priorities. The license block goes first — it gates everything else.

A. License & continuity (ask first)

  • Is Paul staying in the business, or on his way out? (This one answer changes the whole plan.)
  • Are you aware the CSLB license is currently suspended, and that it was Paul's qualifier bond cancelling on 08/15 that did it?
  • Are you performing or collecting on any B-license work right now? (Disgorgement exposure while suspended.)
  • Do you want Paul to stay as qualifier, or do you want to qualify the license yourself?
  • Do you have 4+ years of qualifying experience if you need to sit the exam?
  • Has any customer refused payment or asked for money back citing the license?

B. Entity & tax structure (priority #1 Tax, #3 Asset Protection)

  • Is the LLC taxed as a disregarded entity (Schedule C) or has it made an S-corp election? (Form 568 will tell us.)
  • What was net profit last year, and how much did you take as draw vs. W-2 payroll?
  • Is there a written operating agreement? Who's on it besides you?
  • What happened to the old sole prop (Quality Restoration, 2018) — formally wound down? Assets/customer list moved into the LLC?
  • Do you own the building and equipment personally or in the LLC? Any separate holding entity?
  • Any umbrella/excess policy beyond the $2M GL? Any personal guarantees on debt or leases?
  • Who's your CPA, and when did you last review entity structure with them?

C. Revenue & profitability (priority #4)

  • Actual revenue 2024, 2025, YTD 2026? (Let's settle the $1.1M vs $1.5M.)
  • Split across mitigation, reconstruction, contents, commercial?
  • Do you track job-level profit margins? Who writes your Xactimate estimates?
  • How disciplined is supplement recovery — are you chasing every dollar the carrier owes?

D. Cash flow & AR (the structural pain)

  • Current AR balance and average days-to-pay from carriers?
  • How do you fund payroll and materials during the 60–120 day carrier gap — line of credit, factoring, owner cash?
  • Do you watch a 13-week cash position today? Which carriers/TPAs pay fastest vs. slowest?
  • Any jobs stuck in supplement or depreciation limbo right now?

E. Marketing & lead gen (priority #2 Dashboards + growth)

  • Where do your leads actually come from today — referral, insurance, Yelp, or the web?
  • Did you know your website is compromised and hasn't been meaningfully updated since ~2016–2022?
  • Are you on any carrier DRP/TPA programs? Which ones?
  • Do you run any paid ads (Google LSA, Yelp)? (We found none.)
  • Who owns the relationships with plumbers, agents, and adjusters — you or Paul?
  • Are you routing happy customers to leave Google reviews? (52 on Yelp, thin on Google.)

F. Operations, people & goals

  • How many field crew vs. office, and who does what across the 8–10?
  • Could the business run for a month without you or Paul on site?
  • What numbers do you look at now, if any? (This seeds the dashboard scope.)
  • Equipment owned outright? How well utilized between losses?
  • Three-to-five-year goal — grow, sell, or pass it on? Any succession thinking given the Paul dynamic?
