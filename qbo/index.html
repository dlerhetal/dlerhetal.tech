QBO Wizards

Five step by step walk throughs for QuickBooks Online, written for the person who ends up doing the books because there is nobody else to do them.

No accounting vocabulary is assumed. Where a term of art turns up, it is explained in the same sentence. Each wizard tells you what you need before you start, which mistakes are expensive and which are irreversible, exactly where to click, how to tell it worked, and what to do when it has already gone wrong. Every page works on a phone, prints cleanly, and keeps your own notes in your browser where only you can see them.

These are written for the ordinary version of QuickBooks Online in a browser. Intuit changes menu names from time to time and some paths differ by plan, so anywhere a path could not be stated with certainty, the page says so on the spot rather than guessing.

The wizards

Enter a Bill

About 5 minutes

Record something you owe now and will pay later, so what you owe and when it is due is on record instead of in a pile on the desk. For anyone who has ever paid the same invoice twice.

Open the wizard

Match Downloaded Transactions

15 minutes a week

Clear the queue of bank and credit card transactions QuickBooks downloads, without creating a duplicate of everything you already entered. The most damaging screen in the product if you use it wrong, and the fastest if you use it right.

Open the wizard

Reconcile an Account

15 to 20 minutes a month

Prove your books agree with the bank statement, to the penny, and know what to do when the difference will not go to zero. This is the one thing that makes every other number trustworthy.

Open the wizard

Create and Send an Invoice

About 5 minutes

Bill a customer for work already done and send it from QuickBooks, so you can see when it was opened and chase it on evidence rather than memory.

Open the wizard

Clear Out Aged Receivables

30 minutes to a day

Work the list of money customers owe you: attach payments and credits that were never applied, collect what is still live, and write off what is dead without deleting anything. Usually the highest paid hour in the business.

Do this every week

If you are doing the books yourself, the thing that sinks you is not a wrong click. It is three months of transactions piling up until the file is too far gone to face. A short pass every week and a slightly longer one at month end keeps it from ever getting there, and it is genuinely less total work than a quarterly catch up.

Every week, one sitting

About 30 to 45 minutes. Pick a fixed slot and keep it.

  1. Invoice everything finished this week. Nothing ages faster than an invoice you have not sent yet. Wizard: Create and Send an Invoice. Allow 5 minutes each.
  2. Enter the bills that arrived. Open the envelopes, enter them, attach the PDF, put the paper away. Wizard: Enter a Bill. Allow 3 minutes each once you are used to it.
  3. Clear the downloaded transactions. Match what is already entered, categorize what is not, park anything you do not recognize. Wizard: Match Downloaded Transactions. Allow 15 minutes if you did it last week, an hour if you did not.
  4. Look at who owes you. Run the receivables aging report and make the calls on anything past 30 days. Wizard: Clear Out Aged Receivables, Steps 2, 3 and 7. Allow 10 minutes, plus the calls.

Every month, once the statements arrive

About 1 to 2 hours, on top of that week's normal pass.

  1. Finish the weekly pass first. Reconciling against a half sorted queue wastes your time twice.
  2. Reconcile every account, checking account, savings and every credit card, not just the main one. Wizard: Reconcile an Account. Allow 15 to 20 minutes each once you are current.
  3. Save each reconciliation report as a PDF next to the statement, in the same folder, every month.
  4. Work the receivables properly, not just the calls: apply the unapplied payments and credits, void the duplicates, and write off what is genuinely dead. Wizard: Clear Out Aged Receivables, all steps.
  5. Read your profit report and your balance sheet once the above is done and not before. Until the accounts are reconciled those two reports are opinion, not fact.

If you have already fallen behind, do not try to fix it in date order from the beginning. Get the current month clean first, so that from today forward the file is trustworthy, then work backward a month at a time when you have an evening. A file that is right from today is worth more than one that is half right from two years ago.
