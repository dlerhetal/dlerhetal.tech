1How a position in the box gets won

Google itself names three things that decide who shows up in the map pack and in what order. None of them can be bought directly.

Distance

How far your business is from the person searching, or from the area they typed in.

"How far each business is from the customer who's searching."

Relevance

How well your listing matches what was searched: your categories, the services you list, and the words on your site and profile.

"How well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for."

Prominence

How well-known and well-reviewed your business is: review count, review rating, and how complete your Google Business Profile is.

"More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking."

Source: Google's own local-ranking guidance, support.google.com/business/answer/7091.

Where you already stand on prominence: a 5.0 rating across 21 Google reviews, climbing, and every review answered so far. That is a genuinely strong prominence signal, and it is the hardest one for a competitor to fake. Several competitors in this trade area carry 300 to 700+ reviews, so the honest read is: quality is not the gap, volume is, and volume is a habit, not a redesign.

Where the picture is thin or simply unconfirmed: categories, service-area setup, photo count, hours as configured, and whether Posts or Q&A are being used are all things we cannot see from outside your Google Business Profile dashboard. That is not a finding that they are missing, it is a two-minute check that has not been made yet, and it is listed below because it is free and it is the fastest lever on relevance.

2What moves this, this week, at no cost

  • Fill in every category your Google Business Profile dashboard offers. A primary category of "Roofing contractor" plus every secondary category that genuinely applies (siding, gutters, remodeling) widens which searches you show up for.
  • Turn on service area for your towns. Confirm Albertville, St Michael, Otsego, Monticello, Buffalo, and Elk River are set as service areas inside the profile, not just implied by your address.
  • Post an update when a job wraps. Google Posts are a free feed attached to your listing. A finished job with a photo is a live, current signal that the business is active.
  • Add photos from finished jobs as they happen. Photo count and recency both feed how complete and current your profile looks.
  • Keep replying to every new review. You are already doing this on all 21. Do not let it slip as volume grows, it is one of the cheapest trust signals a homeowner reads before they call.
  • Check hours are accurate inside the profile itself. The website states one set of hours; whether the Google listing matches is a two-minute check worth making once.
