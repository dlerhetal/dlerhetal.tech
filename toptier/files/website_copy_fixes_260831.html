What to change on the live pages

toptiermn.com/services/ and /faq/. Ten changes. Built 31 August 2026 from the live page text.

The whole rule in one sentence: describe what you do to the ROOF, not what you do to the CLAIM. The verb decides it. Inspecting, photographing, measuring, documenting, scoping, replacing and being present are all roofing. Filing, handling, pursuing, representing, advocating, ensuring and confirming coverage are all claim work, and claim work needs a chapter 72B license that a roofing contractor does not have.

What we CAN say

  • We inspect the roof.
  • We photograph and document the condition, slope by slope.
  • We provide a written scope of the roof work and an itemized estimate.
  • We can be on site when the adjuster comes, and walk the roof.
  • We measure the roof and produce a report.
  • We install to manufacturer specification and to code.
  • Our license number is BC807923. (Required on every advertisement, 326B.87.)
  • We report what insurers said, when it is attributed and quoted. "Insurers told Minnesota lawmakers they are moving to actual cash value on older roofs" is reporting.
  • "We can't help you with your policy, but we can help you with your roof."

What we CANNOT say

  • Handle, file, manage, submit or process a claim.
  • Advocacy, advocate, or pursue coverage. Any form of the word.
  • Represent you, or representation, at an adjuster meeting.
  • Ensure, secure, maximize or guarantee coverage.
  • Confirm the cause or the scope of a loss.
  • Tell a homeowner what their policy pays, covers, or owes them.
  • Waive, cover, absorb or discount a deductible, or advertise that you would.
  • "Free roof." Including as an accident of uppercase display type.
  • Say or imply insurers are dropping people over roof age. That is false in Minnesota.
  • Name any service as claim assistance, claim support, or claim assessment.

Line by line

REPLACEServices page, hero line
Now: Top Tier Roofing & Remodeling handles the roof, the siding, and the insurance claim.
Instead: Top Tier Roofing and Remodeling handles the roof and the siding. Your insurer handles the claim, and we make sure they see everything that is up there.
Handling a claim is the regulated activity. Handling a roof is not. The second sentence keeps the whole benefit and gives the claim back to the people licensed to run it.
REPLACEServices page, section heading
Now: Insurance Claim Assistance
Instead: Storm Damage Roof Replacement
A named service called claim assistance is an advertisement for claim services. Name the thing they actually sell instead.
REPLACEServices page, section body
Now: When storms hit, we visit your property, assess the damage, and handle the insurance process to help ensure full coverage.
Instead: When storms hit, we come out, get on the roof, and document the condition slope by slope so there is a complete record of the damage before anyone makes a decision about it.
Handling the insurance process and ensuring coverage are both claim work. Getting on the roof and documenting it is roofing work, and it is the part that actually helps.
REPLACEServices page, service item
Now: Claim Filing Support: Help preparing your claim with the documentation adjusters need.
Instead: Damage Documentation: A photographic record of every slope, and a written scope of the roof work.
Same deliverable, described as what it is. A photo report and a scope are roofing documents. Preparing a claim is claim preparation.
REPLACEServices page, service item
Now: Adjuster Meeting Representation: We meet your adjuster on-site to walk the damage together.
Instead: On Site For The Adjuster Visit: We can be there when your adjuster comes, to walk the roof and point out what we found.
Being present is fine. Representation is a word with a licensing meaning attached to it, and it is the one that turns attendance into advocacy.
REMOVEServices page, service item
Now: Full Coverage Advocacy: If a claim comes back short, we help you pursue the coverage you're owed.
Action: REMOVE THIS ITEM ENTIRELY. Do not replace it.
This is the sharpest line on the page. Advocating a policyholder's coverage is a description of public adjusting, and it uses the word advocacy, which is banned in first person by your own standing rule. There is no compliant rewording, because the service itself is the problem.
REPLACEServices page, button
Now: Start Your Claim Assessment
Instead: Book A Free Inspection
A claim assessment is an assessment of a claim. An inspection is an inspection of a roof.
REPLACEServices page, siding line
Now: Storm Damage Siding Replacement: Full or partial replacement, coordinated with your insurance claim.
Instead: Storm Damage Siding Replacement: Full or partial replacement, on the same schedule as the roof.
Coordinating with a claim is claim administration. Coordinating with the roof schedule is project management.
KEEP, WITH A CHECKServices page, statistic
Now: Minnesota was one of State Farm's top states for hail claims in 2022, according to the Insurance Information Institute.
Action: KEEP, but verify the citation first, and consider replacing it with the Insurance Federation of Minnesota quote already approved for the mailer.
The statistic itself is reporting, which is allowed. But naming a single carrier on a roofer's site is needless, and the Insurance Federation letter is stronger, on the record, and already cleared for use.
REPLACEFAQ page, question and answer
Now: How do I file an insurance claim for storm damage to my roof? You start by documenting the storm damage with photos right away. Top Tier Roofing & Remodeling then confirms the cause and scope before your adjuster visit. Roofing represents nearly 30% of all property claim line items nationally, so this process is common.
Instead: How do I file an insurance claim for storm damage to my roof? Your insurance company and your agent handle the claim itself, and they are the right place to start. What we handle is the roof. After a storm we come out, get on it, and document the condition slope by slope with photographs, so that when your adjuster arrives there is a complete record of what is up there. We can be on site for that visit. We cannot help you with your policy, but we can help you with your roof.
Confirming the cause and scope of a loss is the adjuster's function. Also drop the 30 percent statistic unless a real source can be printed beside it, because an uncited number is its own problem.
Two notes. This is a reading of the statutes, not a legal opinion, and it belongs in front of the attorney you already have a question open with. And nothing here is about the substance of the work: they really do get on the roof, document every slope, and stand there when the adjuster comes. That is a genuine differentiator and it is worth saying loudly. It only breaks when the words move from the roof to the claim.
