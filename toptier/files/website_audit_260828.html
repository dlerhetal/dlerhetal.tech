toptiermn.com: what to fix

Observed directly from the live site, 2026-08-27 · Fixes only, no rebuild
Start with 1 and 2. They are the only two items on this list that change whether a visitor can actually reach you. Everything after them changes whether a visitor finds you in the first place.

The seven fixes, all free, all under a day

1. Nobody can tap the phone number30 minutes
There is not one tel: link anywhere on the site. The phone number is plain text, so on a phone it cannot be tapped to call. Most roofing traffic is mobile.
OBSERVED0 tel: links on the homepage.
2. No way to ask for a quote from the front page1 hour
The homepage has no form at all. There is a single form on the whole site, on a separate quote page a visitor has to find.
OBSERVED0 form elements on the homepage.
3. The license number appears nowhere20 minutes
Minnesota requires the contractor license number on advertising. Theirs is BC807923 and it is absent from the entire site.
OBSERVEDZero occurrences of BC807923 site-wide.
4. The sitemap is broken, and the site tells Google to go look at it10 minutes
The robots file points search engines at a sitemap address that returns “not found.” The SEO plugin that generates it is installed but its sitemap module is switched off. This is a checkbox.
OBSERVEDrobots.txt points to /sitemap_index.xml, which returns 404. /sitemap.xml also 404.
5. Google is told nothing about the business1 hour
There is no local business markup: no phone, no hours, no service area, no rating. The only external profile the site points to is Apple Maps. Their own Google Business Profile is not linked from their own website, and that profile is their strongest asset at 5.0 stars.
OBSERVEDZero occurrences of LocalBusiness markup.
6. The page claims two different towns10 minutes
The page title says St. Michael. The embedded business data says Albertville. Pick the one that matches the Google Business Profile and make everything agree.
OBSERVEDTitle tag and structured data disagree on the city.
7. Half the work they do is invisiblehalf a day
On the homepage, siding, gutter, storm and hail each appear zero times. There is one page covering every service and no page for any individual town.
OBSERVEDsiding 0, gutter 0, storm 0, hail 0 on the homepage.

What this does not need

None of the seven items above requires a rebuild, a new platform, or a designer. Six are settings, text edits or a checkbox. The seventh is writing about work the company already does.
