How to rebuild this list when it goes stale

Wright County publishes the parcel file. The filter that returns the whole commercial universe is: class description begins 3A or 4A, site city in Albertville, St Michael, Buffalo, Monticello or Otsego, total estimated market value under $8,000,000. Sort by owner name and count. Three or more buildings puts an owner on tier 3. Universe: 1,033 commercial buildings under $8M in the hot ZIPs, 87% absentee owned.VERIFIED An independent recount for this document returned 1,041. The gap is a city name filter difference. 1,033 is the figure printed everywhere else in the project and is the figure used here.

Insurance agencies: 30 inside 25 miles

An agency is two targets under one name, and which one is being worked changes who at the agency matters.

AReferral channel

Agents meet homeowners with damaged roofs as a routine part of the job. Who matters: the producer or the agency principal, whoever owns client relationships.

BCommercial roofing customer in its own right

An agency occupies a building. Many of these are small owner occupied offices in the towns already on the card. Who matters: whoever owns or manages the building, which at a small independent agency is often the same principal and at a captive branch is usually a landlord.
