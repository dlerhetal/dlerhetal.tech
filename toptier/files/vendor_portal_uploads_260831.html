The six documents

What a property manager vendor portal asks Top Tier to upload. Built 31 August 2026 from eight verified portals. Tick each one when it is scanned into the folder.

Check number 2 first. Four of these six come from your insurance agent, not from you, and the $2,000,000 umbrella is the one that decides whether several of these portals are open at all. It either exists on the policy or it does not, and finding out takes one phone call. Everything else on this list is paperwork.
1
Certificate of Insurance, on an ACORD formYour insurance agent
General liability $1,000,000. Auto $1,000,000. Personal injury $1,000,000. The property manager named as additional insured.
Several portals will not accept a COI forwarded by the contractor. The agent has to send it directly. Ask for that as a standing instruction.
2
Umbrella policy certificateYour insurance agent
$2,000,000 umbrella over the general liability.
This is the one that stops the whole exercise. Real Property Management Viking names roofing and siding by trade as the work that triggers it. Check before filling in a single form.
3
Waiver of subrogation endorsementYour insurance agent
An endorsement to the general liability policy waiving the insurer's right to recover against the property manager.
Not automatic, and not on a standard policy. It has to be requested and added, and it usually costs something. Most contractors find it missing mid-registration.
4
Workers compensation certificateYour insurance agent, or the state
Coverage per Minnesota statute for anyone on the roof.
Subcontracted crews and no payroll still needs proof, which means an owner exemption filing rather than a policy. A blank here reads as unanswered, not as not applicable.
5
Signed W-9Guy
Legal entity name Top Tier Roofing and Remodeling, LLC, the EIN, the address, signed and dated.
Legal name, not the trading name. A W-9 saying Top Tier Roofing gets bounced against the entity on the COI.
6
Minnesota contractor licenseGuy
A clean scan or PDF of license BC807923, showing the expiration date.
Some portals read the expiration and auto-expire the vendor record when it passes. Diary the renewal.

Two things that are not a document but will stop an upload.

The additional insured wording is different at every company, so there is no single COI that fits all of them. Real Property Management Viking wants XPACE, LLC d/b/a Real Property Management Viking. Sentry Management wants Sentry Management, Inc. and Active Associations. Get the exact wording from each portal before asking the agent, or the certificate comes back and has to be reissued.

Scan everything once, into one folder. Eight portals asking for the same six files is the argument for doing it properly the first time.
