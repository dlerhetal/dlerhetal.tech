Before anything gets posted: the page problem

There are two Top Tier Facebook profiles, and the website links to the wrong one. The live page, facebook.com/profile.php?id=61561195826199, has 230 followers and 4 reviews, carries the tagline and the Instagram handle, and is the one these posts belong on. A second, effectively dead profile, facebook.com/profile.php?id=100054250837723, has 5 followers, 0 reviews and 8 photos. That dead one is what toptiermn.com points at, in both the JSON-LD sameAs block and the article:publisher meta tag.

Two consequences. Anyone who follows the social link from the website lands on an empty profile and leaves. And if one of these posts ever gets boosted, the boost runs from whichever page it was published on, so publishing on the wrong profile spends money building an audience on an account with five followers. Fix the website link to the 230 follower page before the first post goes up.

Instagram is the third loose end. top_tier_rr has 95 posts and is the most content-rich channel the company owns, and it is linked from nowhere on the website. Every graphic in this set is sized for Instagram as well as Facebook, so the same file works on both.

The trapWhat Meta actually saysWhat it costs
Financing turns an ad into a Special Ad Category Since 21 January 2025, US advertisers must declare the Financial products and services category for campaigns promoting loans, long term financing, or 0% APR and in-house installment payments. Roofing ads advertise exactly that, constantly. Declaring it kills ZIP code targeting, kills location exclusions, kills lookalikes, and forces a 15 mile radius around any city or pin. For a company working Albertville, St Michael, Otsego and Buffalo on purpose and staying out of the metro on purpose, a forced 15 mile expansion is expensive. None of the eleven posts mentions financing. Keep it that way in anything promoted.
A lead form may not ask about insurance Meta's lead ads policy forbids form fields that request insurance information without prior written permission, and names two examples directly: asking for a person's insurance company name or details, and asking whether a person is insured. Financial fields are barred too, which rules out a budget bracket question. The single most common roofing instant form question is not allowed. Every boost destination in this set is the website or the message thread, and none is a lead form, partly for this reason. A form can still ask name, phone, email, address, roof age, roof type and what they observed.
