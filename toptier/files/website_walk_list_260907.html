WhatDetail

3The Google Business Profile

Not the website, but the same afternoon’s work, and it feeds the map pack directly. The reasoning behind each of these is in the keywords and free channels guide.

WhatDetail
Fill in the services list properly The highest-value item on this page after the town pages. It is the only profile field Google documents as affecting what you get shown for. Every service actually offered, named the way a customer would say it.
Check the business name is exactly the real name No town, no service words added. Google’s rule says extra words in the name can get the profile suspended, and the profile is what feeds the map pack. Worth two minutes to confirm it is clean.
Never put a phone number in a profile post Google prohibits it. Use the Call now button on the post instead, which uses the verified number anyway. This matters the week the mail lands, because a phone number is exactly what you will want to type.
Do not post your own questions and answer them Answering a real customer’s question is fine and worth doing. Seeding your own falls under the fake-engagement rule. Put frequently asked questions on the website instead.
Add recent photographs Real jobs, recent dates. Cheapest credibility available and most competitors neglect it.
Claim the free Bing and Apple listings Both free. Apple Maps is what every iPhone opens, and Bing feeds Copilot’s local answers.
