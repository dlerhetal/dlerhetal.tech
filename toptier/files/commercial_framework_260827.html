What this is, and what it is not

This is the operating framework behind the commercial card that is already on your wall. The card names the customer and the four ways to reach them. This document turns each of those into something a person can actually do on a Tuesday, and it puts the target list, the record keeping and the scoreboard behind it.

It is written to be run by you, not by us. Every list in here has its recipe printed next to it, so when the list goes stale you rebuild it yourself instead of asking anyone for a new one.

The spine: the five steps, made operational

Each step carries the same four things: what it means in practice, which seat owns it, what goes into Contractors Cloud, and what finished looks like. Seats, not names. Any of you can sit in any seat, but only one seat owns a step at a time.

1WHO? Qualify before you spend anything on them

The card's own gate, unchanged: location is local and specifically not Minneapolis, size is under 100,000 sq ft, and it is not commercial steel.

What it means operationally. A target passes the gate or it comes off the list. There is no maybe pile. Two additions come out of the parcel data and are worth adopting because they cost nothing to check:

  • Absentee owned. 87.2% of the commercial universe in your towns is owned by somebody whose mailing address is not the building.VERIFIED An absentee owner already pays somebody else to make the roof decision, which means a decision maker exists and can be found.
  • Three or more buildings. One relationship, several roofs. 33 owners in your towns hold three or more.VERIFIED

Teach yourself the pull. Wright County publishes the parcel file. 2Walk in

What it means operationally. Physically entering the office of a management company or a portfolio owner during business hours, with something to leave behind. This is the highest yield method on the card for one reason: property management is a relationship trade, and managers hire vendors they have met. It is also the slowest per contact, so it is spent only on the top of the list.

What you leave behind matters more than what happens in the room, because the person who decides may not be the person at the desk. The leave behind carries, at minimum: company name and phone, the license number BC807923, what you do, and the towns you cover. One page. Nothing that mentions insurance coverage or claims.

The objective of the visit is the vendor application. Every management company of any size keeps an approved vendor list, and getting onto it is a form, not a sale. If there is no form, the objective is the name and direct number of whoever approves vendors.

3Email

What it means operationally. The cheapest method and the one that scales, which is exactly why it is the one that gets ignored when it is sent cold and empty. Email earns its place here as the carrier of a document, not as an introduction. The document is the offer below.

The offer that fits this buyer: a written roof condition report. A property manager's job is defending a budget to an owner or a board. A free estimate asks them to buy something. A written condition report with photographs and a plain statement of what the roof needs, and when, hands them the document that gets their money approved. That is the thing being sold first. The roof follows it.INFERENCE

It also plays to a capability the company already has and already uses on every job, which is photographs and documentation.

Every email that offers or advertises anything carries BC807923. An email offering services is advertising under 326B.87.

5Mailers

What it means operationally. The only method on the card that reaches a target you cannot get to any other way, which on this list is the association board. A condominium or common interest community is governed by a board, and the board is reachable by letter at the association's registered address or through its management company. An individual unit owner cannot buy a roof in one of these. The association does.

Commercial mail is not the residential mailer, and must not be run off the same list. The residential mailer is a saturation buy against roof age. Commercial mail here is a small, named, addressed list. Different piece, different list, different scoreboard row.

Repetition is what makes mail work. Nobody responds to a roofing piece the first time it appears, in any market. Plan the same small list twice rather than a list twice the size once.INFERENCE

The legal frame, and it is short

Three lines that govern everything above.
  • Minnesota Statute 326B.87. The license number goes on every piece of advertising. BC807923. Leave behind, email footer, mailer, vehicle, web page, all of it.
  • Minnesota Statute 325E.66 subdivision 1(a)(3). A contractor may not interpret policy provisions or advise on coverage unless licensed under chapter 72B. Penalties reach $10,000 per violation.
  • Minnesota Statute 72B.02 subdivision 6(2). Advertising or holding yourself out as able to handle a claim is itself the regulated act. The advertisement, not just the conduct.

What that means practically. Nothing produced under this framework mentions insurance, claims, coverage or deductibles. The condition report describes the roof, not the policy. Where a commercial prospect turns out to have storm damage with a carrier involved, that conversation goes behind an attorney gate before it goes anywhere else, and it is handled as scope documentation or as cash pay work, never as advice about what a policy will pay. Statute text at https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/325E.66 and https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/326B.87.

What to record, and why this section is the whole ball game

You have never measured a non-referral channel. That is the single most important fact in this document, and it makes the recording harder to get right than the selling.

159 of 161 projects in Contractors Cloud say Referral.VERIFIED That is not a description of where the work came from, it is a default nobody changed. It means that if commercial outreach produces a job tomorrow and nothing changes in the CRM, that job will also say Referral, and the entire channel will be invisible six months from now when somebody asks whether it was worth doing.

The proof that this is fixable, from your own data. Cancel Reason is filled on 34 of 34 records.VERIFIED Not because anyone is disciplined about it, but because the dialog will not close without it. You fill what the workflow forces. The fix for lead source is the same shape: make the field required at record creation, with a pick list of sources that actually exist.
One constraint worth knowing before anyone tries. The Contractors Cloud API exposes lead sources as read only. New lead sources have to be created by an owner in the user interface, they cannot be pushed in.VERIFIED A settings change, not a project, but a settings change somebody has to sit down and make.
And the honest limit: this starts now and cannot be backfilled. Coding the 159 historical records would be inventing history. Lead source starts accruing value from the next lead in and never before. Every day the field waits is data that is permanently gone.

What is not known, stated so nobody fills the gap with a number

1. Two jobs are not a segment

There is no commercial close rate, no commercial average ticket and no commercial margin anywhere in this business, because there are two commercial records in the entire history of the company and one of them does not appear in the CRM at all.VERIFIED

Anyone who quotes one of those numbers made it up. That includes us, it includes any vendor who pitches you, and it will include your own instinct after the first commercial job lands, because one data point feels like a pattern and is not. The scoreboard is how those numbers get created honestly, and the first credible close rate is several sales away.

2. Commercial roof age cannot be targeted from parcel data

98.2% of commercial parcels in your towns carry no Year Built at all.VERIFIED, 1,022 of 1,041 The residential targeting method, which sorts neighborhoods by build year to find roofs at end of life, does not transfer to commercial and cannot be made to.

Do not build a roof age play for commercial. The substitute is the condition report itself: on commercial you find out how old the roof is by going and looking at it, which is exactly why the offer in step 3 is a report rather than a mailer. The information you would have bought from data has to be earned by a visit.

Worth saying out loud on the residential side too. Year Built is not roof age there either. It is a proxy, and a house re-roofed in 2019 sits wrongly on any build year list.

3. The things nobody has asked yet
  • Does a commercial job get paid on the same terms as residential?ASK Commercial collection cycles are typically longer and the deposit convention is different. The current model is a 50% deposit. Whether that survives contact with a property manager is unknown, and it is a cash question before it is a sales question.
  • What is on the flat and low slope roofs?ASK A meaningful share of small commercial is not shingle. Whether that work is done in house, subcontracted, or passed on has to be settled before the first walk in, because it changes which buildings qualify.
  • Who covers the commercial pipeline when the sales seat is on a roof?ASK This is the same open question that governs the residential channel. Outbound only compounds it.
  • What is the actual footprint of the buildings?INFERENCE only The card's 100,000 sq ft test is applied here through lot acreage, which bounds the building but does not measure it. No building square footage field exists in the parcel data.
  • The three blank bullets on the card.ASK The commercial sheet has three empty dashes under the customer definition. Whatever was going to go there was never written down, and if it was a third or fourth customer type it belongs in this framework.
4. What this framework deliberately does not do
  • It does not tell you what to say. No scripts, no openers, no call order. You have been selling roofs in these towns for years and the framework has nothing to add on that. It gives you the target, the sequence, the number and the thing to write down.
  • It does not run the channel for you. Every list in here has its recipe printed beside it so you can rebuild it. The measure of whether this worked is whether you can produce the next version of the target list without asking anyone.
  • It does not touch insurance. See the legal frame. That is not caution, it is statute.
  • It does not put a date on anything. Sequence and dependency only. The chain is: qualify, then set up the recording, then contact, then measure, then decide where to spend.
