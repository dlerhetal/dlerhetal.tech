Step 1 of 3

DKIM — sign outgoing mail

DKIM stamps every outgoing email with a signature that proves it really came from your domain's mail servers (Google's, in this case). This is the biggest single fix for the junk-folder problem.
Open Google Admin's Authenticate Email page
Sign in as the super admin for toptiermn.com.
Open Google Admin ↗
Select toptiermn.com, click "Generate new record"
Choose 2048-bit key length when it asks (stronger, and Google's own recommendation). Leave this tab open — it shows a host name and a long TXT value you need next.
Open GoDaddy's DNS page for toptiermn.com
In a second tab, alongside the Google Admin one.
Open GoDaddy DNS ↗
Add a new TXT record with what Google gave you
Type: TXT · Name: the host name shown on the Google tab (something like google._domainkey) · Value: the long v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=... string. Save.
Wait 15–30 minutes, then start authentication
Back on the Google Admin tab, click Start authentication. If it fails, the DNS record probably hasn't propagated yet — wait a bit longer and try again.
