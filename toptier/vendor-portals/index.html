The folder you are working from

  1. ACORD certificate of insurance
  2. $2,000,000 umbrella confirmation
  3. Waiver of subrogation endorsement
  4. Workers compensation certificate
  5. Signed W-9
  6. Minnesota license BC807923

Everything below is scored against those six. Where a portal asks for something that is not on this list, it is called out under Also wants.

The $2,000,000 umbrella question, answered. Only one company on this entire list publishes a hard umbrella requirement: Real Property Management Viking, whose vendor manual requires a $2,000,000 umbrella and names roofing and siding as the trades it applies to. Gassen publishes full limits and does not ask for an umbrella at all. Every other requirement list is either silent on dollar figures or sits behind a platform login, so no honest claim can be made about them. The practical read: carry the $2,000,000 and nothing on this list is closed off. Confirm the number on the certificate before the first upload, because it is the slowest thing to fix.
