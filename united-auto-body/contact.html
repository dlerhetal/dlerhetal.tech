Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes — we work with all insurance companies and deal directly with your adjuster. You always have the right to choose your own repair shop; it doesn't have to be one your insurer "recommends."

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation written estimates, and the price we quote is the price you pay.

Is your work guaranteed?

We back most repairs with a limited lifetime warranty for as long as you own the vehicle.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed weekends.
