Complete collision & auto body services in Issaquah

For over 40 years, Issaquah and the Eastside have trusted United Auto Body to restore their vehicles — safely, correctly, and on every make and model.

What we repair

Done right the first time, backed for life

Every repair is restored to pre-accident condition and covered by our limited lifetime warranty. We work with all insurance companies and handle the claim for you.

01

Collision Repair

From minor fender-benders to major structural damage, we return your vehicle to factory condition on all makes and models. Our technicians follow proper repair procedures so your car is safe, not just good-looking.

02

Frame & Structural Repair

Computerized measuring and frame straightening for both steel and aluminum vehicles, bringing your vehicle back to factory specifications and protecting its crash integrity.

03

Auto Body Painting & Color Matching

Expert refinishing with computerized, camera-matched color so repairs blend seamlessly. We use environmentally friendly paints — better for your car and for Issaquah.

04

Paintless Dent Repair (PDR)

For dings, hail, and minor dents that haven't broken the paint, PDR restores the panel fast and affordably while preserving your original factory finish.

05

Motorcycle Collision Repair

Specialized bodywork and refinishing for motorcycles — a niche many shops won't touch, and one we've handled for decades.

06

Auto Glass, Lights & Trim

Glass, headlights and taillights, power windows and locks, and interior & exterior trim — the details that make a repair complete.

07

Insurance Claim Assistance

You choose your repair shop — never the insurance company. We work directly with your adjuster on the estimate and supplements so your claim is paid fairly, and keep you updated throughout.

08

Free Pre-Purchase Inspections

Buying a used car? Bring it in and we'll inspect the body and structure for hidden prior damage before you commit — free of charge.
