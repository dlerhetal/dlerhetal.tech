Our story

Four decades of honest work in Issaquah.

United Auto Body has been repairing the Eastside's vehicles since 1982. We're a family-owned, independent shop — which means our only loyalty is to you, not to an insurance company's bottom line.

Customers come back (and send their friends) because we tell the truth about what a car needs, do the work right, and stand behind it with a limited lifetime warranty. We even use environmentally friendly paints, because doing it right means doing right by Issaquah.
