Bookkeeping Operations

A private financial-operations tool used to diagnose and correct accounting records inside QuickBooks Online, on behalf of the businesses that engage us.

This application connects to QuickBooks Online strictly through Intuit's official API using OAuth 2.0. It reads ledger, balance-sheet, and transaction data, and — only with the account owner's authorization — posts corrections back to fix bookkeeping errors such as miscategorized transactions, duplicate entries, and incorrect opening balances. It is not a public sign-up product; access is provisioned per engagement.

Connecting

To connect or reconnect, the QuickBooks Online account owner (or an authorized admin) completes Intuit's standard "Connect to QuickBooks" authorization. This grants the application access to the specific company file the owner selects. If you were engaged for a cleanup, you'll receive a connect link directly from us.

  1. Open the connect link provided to you.
  2. Sign in to QuickBooks Online and choose the company to connect.
  3. Review and approve the requested accounting access.

Disconnecting

You can revoke this application's access at any time — access ends immediately when you do.

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps (or Connected Apps).
  3. Find this application and select Disconnect.

You may also email dale@linn.net and we will disconnect and delete stored tokens on request.
